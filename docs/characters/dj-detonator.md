## Personality

Big Dez's DJ, heard over the club's PA rather than on the radio. A hype man who treats the minesweeping as part of the show: shout-outs, crowd work, requests from the tables. He never takes the mines seriously and never slows the music down.

## Quirks

- **Shout-outs:** to the Sweeper "clicking tiles in the middle of the dance floor" (`dj_1`) and to Mom, who is on the guest list (`dj_5`).
- **Safety announcements as crowd calls:** "Put your hands up! But not on the tiles!" (`dj_2`).
- **Requests denied:** table six asked for fewer explosions (`dj_3`).
- **"We never slow it down."** (`dj_4`) His concept-art note; the club theme is a fixed 124 BPM.

## Story arc

He exists only in [Da Club](../levels/3-da-club.md). He opens the chapter's chatter (`dj_1`) and shares the favourite weight with Dez, so he is one of the two main voices there. His `dj_5` confirms from the outside that Mom really did come to the club (`mc_2`). The scenery's DJ in the booth, bobbing on the beat with headphones, is him.
