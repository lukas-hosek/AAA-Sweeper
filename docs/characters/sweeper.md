# The Sweeper

The player character. Never speaks, never seen, no name beyond the call sign everyone uses: "Sweeper". The kill feed calls them SWEEPER; rank titles run from RECRUIT to SWEEPLORD.

## Who they are, according to everyone else

The Sweeper is defined entirely by what the cast says to them, which is the AAA silent-protagonist trope.

- **To Command (Reyes):** an asset with regenerating health and a performance review next week (`rg_1`).
- **To Dr. Kova:** an old colleague she double-crossed, and a subscription customer. The neural interface is in the Sweeper's head.
- **To Big Dez:** a friend who never shows up; "The bouncer thinks you're a myth." Then a legend, VIP for life.
- **To Mom:** her child, who burns easily (`md_1`), can't swim very well (`mp_1`), hasn't answered the wedding invitation (`m_2`) and hasn't called in eleven days (Call Your Mother).
- **To Jenkins:** a hero; he is the president of their one-member fan club.
- **To the whisper:** someone the field is clearing (`w_3`).

## Their story

Sent from field to field by Command, chased by calls from everyone they know, the Sweeper clears a battlefield, a desert, a nightclub, an Arctic glacier and a flooding underwater base, then goes home to Mom's backyard for dinner, where the whole cast gathers. The credits promise "SWEEPER WILL RETURN"; the campaign continues in New Game+.

No line states the Sweeper's gender.
